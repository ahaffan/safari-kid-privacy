Komitmen Privasi Arkananta Horizon:
Aplikasi Safari Kid (Package: com.arkanantahorizon.safarikid) menghormati hak privasi Anda sepenuhnya. Pengguna berhak meminta dan mengeksekusi penghapusan akun serta seluruh data yang terkait kapan saja, tanpa syarat yang memberatkan.

1. 📱 Cara Menghapus Akun & Data Anda

Anda dapat menghapus akun dan data pribadi anak Anda melalui salah satu metode berikut:

Metode 1: Langsung di Dalam Aplikasi (Paling Cepat & Seketika)

  1. Buka aplikasi Safari Kid di ponsel Android Anda.
  2. Buka menu Pengaturan (Settings / Akun) di bilah navigasi bawah.
  3. Gulir ke bawah hingga bagian "Data & Privasi".
  4. Ketuk tombol "Hapus Semua Data / Reset Data".
  5. Konfirmasikan dialog peringatan dengan menekan "Ya, Hapus Permanen".
  6. Seluruh profil anak, riwayat kurva pertumbuhan Z-score, dan foto scrapbook akan langsung terhapus seketika dari penyimpanan lokal perangkat Anda.

Metode 2: Melalui Pengaturan Sistem Android

  1. Buka Pengaturan (Settings) perangkat Android Anda.
  2. Pilih Aplikasi (Apps / Application Manager) > Cari dan pilih Safari Kid.
  3. Pilih Penyimpanan (Storage & Cache).
  4. Ketuk Hapus Penyimpanan (Clear Storage / Clear Data).
  5. Semua data lokal aplikasi akan langsung terhapus bersih dari ponsel Anda.

Metode 3: Permintaan Penghapusan Manual Melalui Email Pengembang

Jika Anda telah menghapus aplikasi dan ingin memastikan tidak ada data atau sesi akun tertinggal, Anda dapat mengirimkan permintaan penghapusan tertulis kepada tim pengembang kami:

  • Email Tujuan: affan@arkananta.id atau support@arkananta.id
  • Subjek Email: Permintaan Penghapusan Data Safari Kid - [Nama / Alamat Email Anda]
  • Informasi yang Disertakan: Alamat email yang digunakan saat masuk atau Kode Unik Pengguna (misal: GTK-USR-XXXXX jika Anda mengingatnya).
  • Waktu Pemrosesan: Tim Arkananta Horizon akan memverifikasi dan menyelesaikan permohonan dalam waktu 1 x 24 jam kerja dan mengirimkan konfirmasi tertulis.

Arkananta Horizon Privacy Commitment:
The application Safari Kid (Package: com.arkanantahorizon.safarikid) strictly respects your privacy rights. Users can request and perform account and data deletion at any time without unnecessary barriers.

1. 📱 How to Delete Your Account & Associated Data

You can delete your account and child growth records through any of the following methods:

Method 1: Directly In-App (Instant & Real-Time)

  1. Open the Safari Kid application on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings (Account) screen in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the "Data & Privacy" section.
  4. Tap the "Reset All Data / Delete Account" button.
  5. Confirm the dialog prompt by tapping "Yes, Delete Permanently".
  6. All child profiles, WHO Z-score growth curves, and scrapbook photos will be permanently wiped immediately from your local device storage.

Method 2: Via Android System Settings

  1. Open Android Settings on your device.
  2. Select Apps > Find and tap Safari Kid.
  3. Select Storage & Cache.
  4. Tap Clear Storage (Clear Data).
  5. All application files and databases will be completely erased from your phone.

Method 3: Written Request via Developer Email

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to formally ensure zero metadata remains, submit a request directly to our developer desk:

  • Recipient: affan@arkananta.id or support@arkananta.id
  • Email Subject: Safari Kid Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / Email]
  • Required Information: The email address used during login or your Unique User Code (e.g. GTK-USR-XXXXX).
  • Resolution Timeframe: Requests are processed within 24 business hours followed by written confirmation.

3. ⏱️ Data Retention & Zero Retention Guarantee

Zero-Cloud Retention:
Because Safari Kid is built with strict on-device privacy principles, we DO NOT RETAIN any copies of your child’s health records, photos, or credentials on external cloud servers after deletion. Once executed, deletion is instantaneous and irreversible.
